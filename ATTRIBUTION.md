# Attribution

ETHGlobal require documentation of "which parts of the code, specific files, or
assets were generated or assisted by AI." This file is that record. It is
maintained from the first commit onward, per file, as work lands — not
reconstructed at the end.

## Summary

**This build is AI-assisted throughout.** Claude Code (Claude Opus 5) wrote the
large majority of the implementation, directed by PhantomOz through a
spec-driven workflow. All specs, prompts and planning artifacts are committed in
`/specs` and `/prompts` so the direction is inspectable, not just the output.

Human contribution is concentrated in the places it belongs: problem definition,
architectural judgment, the track-requirements analysis that shaped scope, the
decisions recorded in `DECISIONS.md`, review of every change, and the demo
video — which is narrated by a team member and is not AI-generated.

## Legend

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|

| `AI+H` | Generated by Claude Code, then materially edited by a team member |
| `H` | Written by a team member |
| `H+AI` | Written by a team member with AI assistance on parts |

## Commit trailers

Commits carry `Co-Authored-By` trailers naming the AI model. Those trailers are
disclosure, not the attribution mechanism — this file is. Commit *authorship*
is the team member who directed and reviewed the work.
